### Key insight from the arc

Teacher's validation shifted from **"did AI get the right answer?"** to **"how does AI reason about being wrong?"** — the meta-quality (self-reflection precision) was more impressive to her than object-level correctness.

**Reflection**: for teachers with decades of student experience, "students who can learn from mistakes with precision" is the top-tier trait. AI arrived at this trait through 3 rounds of Ethan pushing "reflect more precisely, less big-principle" — see `meta_lessons.md` #2.
